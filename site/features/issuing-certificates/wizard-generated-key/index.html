Issue with a generated key

Let the wizard give you the openssl command so the key never leaves your machine.

As pki-admin

  1. Step 1

    Open /certificates/issue

  2. Step 2

    Select "Acme Issuing CA" in Certificate authority

  3. Step 3

    Click Next

  4. Step 4

    Select "Web Server (90 day)" in Issuance template

  5. Step 5

    Click Next

  6. Step 6

    Fill Subject common name with "gen.acme.test"

  7. Step 7

    Fill Subject alternative names with "gen.acme.test"

  8. Step 8

    Click Next

  9. Step 9

    Click Generate a new key
