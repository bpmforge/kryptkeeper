## Observations

- Single chunk, no code splitting (LOW, L1). `grep -rn "lazy(" ui/src` finds none, and `src/pages` holds 37
  page components that are all statically imported by the router. Vite warns "Some chunks are larger than
  500 kB after minification". Every user downloads and parses all pages, including rarely used ones
  (Tenants, Spiffe, CodeSigning, PQCMigration, ComplianceEvidence). Route-level `React.lazy` would likely halve
  the first-load JS; not measured.
- MSW (the mock service worker) is only started under `import.meta.env.DEV` (`ui/src/main.tsx:8`) and is not in
  the production bundle (no `msw` strings found in the output).
- Runtime cost is dominated by the server, not the bundle: the Dashboard issues up to 10 sequential
  `GET /certificates?limit=100` calls to build its expiry horizon (`ui/src/pages/Dashboard.tsx:286-307`),
  each of which costs 30 ms / 31 MB server-side at 50k certificates, plus the summary, CA and audit calls.
  Polling is modest: Agents and Approvals every 30 s, Discovery 4 s only while a scan is active.
- The generated `src/generated/api-types.ts` was regenerated by `prebuild` with no diff against the tree.
