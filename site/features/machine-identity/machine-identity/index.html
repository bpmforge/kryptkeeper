Propose and approve a machine identity

One person proposes a machine identity and another approves it.

Proposer side

As pki-admin

  1. Step 1

    Open /nhi

  2. Step 2

    Click Propose identity

  3. Step 3

    Fill Name with "billing-bot"

  4. Step 4

    Select "service_account" in select

  5. Step 5

    Tick certificate.read

  6. Step 6

    Click Propose

Approver side

As approver

  1. Step 1

    Open /nhi

  2. Step 2

    Click Approve billing-bot
