Escrow and recover a root key

Open the escrow, recover and BYOK-export actions for a root key, which need two people to complete.

As pki-admin

  1. Step 1

    Open /keys

  2. Step 2

    Click Escrow

  3. Step 3

    Press Escape to close the dialog

  4. Step 4

    Click Recover

  5. Step 5

    Press Escape to close the dialog

  6. Step 6

    Click BYOK export

  7. Step 7

    Press Escape to close the dialog

  8. Step 8

    Open /reports/key-custody
