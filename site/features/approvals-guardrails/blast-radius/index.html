Check the blast radius first

See every certificate and service a destructive change would affect.

As pki-admin

  1. Step 1

    Open /blast-radius

  2. Step 2

    Fill Target with the certificate's SHA-256 fingerprint

  3. Step 3

    Click Simulate

  4. Step 4

    Select "Retire CA" in Action

  5. Step 5

    Fill Target with the CA id

  6. Step 6

    Click Simulate

  7. Step 7

    Select "Deprecate algorithm" in Action

  8. Step 8

    Fill Algorithm and size with "RSA-2048"

  9. Step 9

    Click Simulate
