Run a discovery scan

Scan a range and read what was found.

As pki-admin

  1. Step 1

    Open /discovery

  2. Step 2

    Click New scan

  3. Step 3

    Fill the scan target with the host to scan

  4. Step 4

    Fill e.g. 9443, 8888 with "18490,18491,18492,18493"

  5. Step 5

    Click Queue scan

  6. Step 6

    Wait for the scan to finish

  7. Step 7

    Click Refresh

  8. Step 8

    Turn on Show risky endpoints only
