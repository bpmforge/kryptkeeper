Create the issuing CA for the domain

Create the intermediate CA that will sign your domain certificates.

As pki-admin

  1. Step 1

    Open /cas

  2. Step 2

    Click New CA

  3. Step 3

    Select "Intermediate" in Type

  4. Step 4

    Fill Subject CN with "Lab Issuing CA"

  5. Step 5

    Choose the parent CA (here Lab Root CA)

  6. Step 6

    Choosing Intermediate reveals the Parent CA picker

  7. Step 7

    Click Create CA
