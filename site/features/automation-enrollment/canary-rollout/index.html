Create a rollout group for a cluster

Group a cluster's nodes, set a rollout window and save the group.

As pki-admin

  1. Step 1

    Open /renewal/rollout-groups

  2. Step 2

    Click New group

  3. Step 3

    Fill Group name with "web-cluster"

  4. Step 4

    Tick web-01

  5. Step 5

    Tick web-02

  6. Step 6

    Fill Window start with "2"

  7. Step 7

    Fill Window end with "4"

  8. Step 8

    Click Save group

  9. Step 9

    Click web-cluster
