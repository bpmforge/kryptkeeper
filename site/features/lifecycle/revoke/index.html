Revoke certificates

Revoke one or many certificates with a reason.

As pki-admin

  1. Step 1

    Open /certificates

  2. Step 2

    Click checkout-v2.svc.acme.test

  3. Step 3

    Click Revoke

  4. Step 4

    Fill Optional explanation with "Disposable test certificate"

  5. Step 5

    Fill REVOKE with "REVOKE"

  6. Step 6

    Click Confirm revoke

  7. Step 7

    Open /certificates

  8. Step 8

    Tick Select vpn-gateway.svc.acme.test

  9. Step 9

    Click Bulk revoke

  10. Step 10

    Open /reports/revocation-activity
