Send a test delivery and build alert rules

Send a test delivery, open its detail, then write an alert rule.

As pki-admin

  1. Step 1

    Open /alerts

  2. Step 2

    Select "Email" in Notification test channel

  3. Step 3

    Fill Notification test target with "ops@acme.test"

  4. Step 4

    Click Send test delivery

  5. Step 5

    Click a delivery row to open its detail, attempts and actions

  6. Step 6

    Click Alert rules

  7. Step 7

    Click New rule

  8. Step 8

    Fill Name with "Expiry 14d to platform"

  9. Step 9

    Fill Target with "platform@acme.test"

  10. Step 10

    Click Create rule
