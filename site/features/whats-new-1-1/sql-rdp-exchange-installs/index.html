Install onto SQL Server, Remote Desktop or Exchange

Declare a Windows target and choose a WinRM install mode, with the credential stored here or fetched from a secret manager.

As pki-admin

  1. Step 1

    Open /targets/setup

  2. Step 2

    Click Agentless

  3. Step 3

    Fill Target name with "sql-01"

  4. Step 4

    Fill Host with "sql-01.acme.test"

  5. Step 5

    Click Windows

  6. Step 6

    Click Next

  7. Step 7

    Click Next

  8. Step 8

    Select "Microsoft SQL Server (WinRM)" in Install mode

  9. Step 9

    Click Next

  10. Step 10

    Select "+ Create a new credential" in Credential

  11. Step 11

    Click Fetch it from a secret manager

  12. Step 12

    Click Store it here

  13. Step 13

    Fill Name with "sql-01 WinRM login"

  14. Step 14

    Fill WinRM endpoint with "http://sql-01.acme.test:5985/wsman"

  15. Step 15

    Fill Username with "ACME\svc-pki"

  16. Step 16

    Fill Password

  17. Step 17

    Fill Instance name with "SQLEXPRESS"

  18. Step 18

    Click Create & use

  19. Step 19

    Click Next

  20. Step 20

    Click Finish
