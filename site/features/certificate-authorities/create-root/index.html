Create a root CA

Create a self-signed root with the algorithm and lifetime you choose.

As pki-admin

  1. Step 1

    Open Certificate Authorities

  2. Step 2

    Click New CA

  3. Step 3

    Select "Root" in Type

  4. Step 4

    Fill Subject CN with "Lab Root CA"

  5. Step 5

    Fill Subject Org with "Acme"

  6. Step 6

    Select "ECDSA P-384" in Algorithm

  7. Step 7

    Fill Validity with "10y"

  8. Step 8

    The filled New Certificate Authority dialog

  9. Step 9

    Click Create CA
