Configure notification channels

Set up email delivery and a webhook, then send a test message.

As pki-admin

  1. Step 1

    Open /integrations

  2. Step 2

    Fill SMTP host with your mail relay

  3. Step 3

    Click Save on the SMTP host row

  4. Step 4

    Fill SMTP port with "11025"

  5. Step 5

    Click Save on the SMTP port row

  6. Step 6

    Fill SMTP from address with "kryptkeeper@acme.test"

  7. Step 7

    Click Save on the SMTP from address row

  8. Step 8

    Fill Notification test target with "ops@acme.test"

  9. Step 9

    Click Send test

  10. Step 10

    The sink: the test message arrives in the Mailpit inbox
