Find anything from the keyboard

Use global search and the command palette to jump straight to a certificate or page.

As pki-admin

  1. Step 1

    Open the Dashboard

  2. Step 2

    Press Ctrl/Cmd+K to open the command palette

  3. Step 3

    Fill the palette search with "acme"

  4. Step 4

    Press Escape to close the palette

  5. Step 5

    Open Search without a query

  6. Step 6

    Fill Search all inventory with "acme"

  7. Step 7

    Click Clear
