Push a CA to your hosts' trust stores

Propose a CA bundle for chosen agents, have a second person approve it, and read the per-host result.

As pki-admin

  1. Step 1

    Open /agents

  2. Step 2

    Fill Name with "Acme Vault PKI Root"

  3. Step 3

    Paste the CA certificate (certificates only, never keys)

  4. Step 4

    Check Every Linux agent

  5. Step 5

    Fill Why for the approver

  6. Step 6

    Click Propose for approval

  7. Step 7

    A second administrator approves the bundle

  8. Step 8

    Open /agents again

  9. Step 9

    Click the bundle to see each host
