Start a post-quantum migration wave

Pick the certificates, an ML-DSA template and a target algorithm, then propose the wave for approval.

As pki-admin

  1. Step 1

    Open the app

  2. Step 2

    Setup: a template that allows ML-DSA

  3. Step 3

    Open /reports/pqc-migration

  4. Step 4

    Click Start migration wave

  5. Step 5

    Fill Wave name with "internal-services-wave-1"

  6. Step 6

    Fill Owner with "platform-team"

  7. Step 7

    Click Next

  8. Step 8

    Check notifier.svc.acme.test

  9. Step 9

    Check registry.acme.test

  10. Step 10

    Click Next

  11. Step 11

    Select "Web Server, ML-DSA (90 day)" in Issuance template

  12. Step 12

    Select "ML-DSA-65" in Target algorithm

  13. Step 13

    Click Next

  14. Step 14

    Click Finish
