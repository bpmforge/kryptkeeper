Feature

Renewal and revocation

Own every certificate, renew in bulk, revoke in one step.

Take ownership of certificates nobody owned, import the ones you already have, and renew or revoke one at a time or in bulk. Revocation is published over OCSP and CRL.

How it works

  1. Take ownership of an unmanaged certificate

    Claim a discovered certificate, record who owns it, then take over several at once.

    10 steps

  2. Bulk import existing certificates

    Import a batch of certificates you already have.

    5 steps

  3. Renew certificates

    Renew a single certificate or a whole selection at once.

    10 steps

  4. Revoke certificates

    Revoke one or many certificates with a reason.

    10 steps
