Generate, back up and rotate keys

Run the key lifecycle from the console.

As pki-admin

  1. Step 1

    Open /keys

  2. Step 2

    Click Generate Key

  3. Step 3

    Fill e.g. root-ca-2026 with "lab-signing-key"

  4. Step 4

    Select "ECDSA-P384" in select

  5. Step 5

    Click Generate

  6. Step 6

    Click Import

  7. Step 7

    Click Escrow

  8. Step 8

    Tick Select lab-signing-key

  9. Step 9

    Bulk rotate and Bulk delete are destructive and are captured only up to the button
