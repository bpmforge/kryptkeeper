Alerts and notifications

Hear about expiry before your users do.

Send alerts to email, Slack, webhooks or PagerDuty, build rules for what matters, and open tickets in Jira or ServiceNow.

How it works

    Set up email delivery and a webhook, then send a test message.

  2. Send a test delivery and build alert rules

    Send a test delivery, open its detail, then write an alert rule.

    10 steps

  3. Connect Jira or ServiceNow

    Choose Jira or ServiceNow and enter the connection details for ticket-based alerts.

    5 steps
