Keep the CA you already use

Connect HashiCorp Vault PKI as an issuer, test it, and propose it as an external CA for a second administrator to approve.

As pki-admin

  1. Step 1

    Open /issuers

  2. Step 2

    Click Add issuer

  3. Step 3

    Pick HashiCorp Vault PKI

  4. Step 4

    Click Next

  5. Step 5

    Fill Issuer name with "Acme Vault PKI"

  6. Step 6

    Fill Vault address with "https://localhost:18321"

  7. Step 7

    Fill Role with "acme-web"

  8. Step 8

    Paste the Vault server CA certificate

  9. Step 9

    Click Next

  10. Step 10

    Fill Vault token

  11. Step 11

    Click Next

  12. Step 12

    Click Save and test

  13. Step 13

    Click Next

  14. Step 14

    Fill CA name with "Acme Vault PKI CA"

  15. Step 15

    Paste the Vault root as the trust PEM

  16. Step 16

    Click Finish

  17. Step 17

    Open /inbox

  18. Step 18

    The new external CA waits here for a second administrator
