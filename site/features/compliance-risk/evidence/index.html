Produce compliance evidence

Build an evidence pack or CBOM, or schedule it.

  1. Step 1

    Open /reports/compliance-evidence

  2. Step 2

    Select "SOC 2" in Compliance framework

  3. Step 3

    Select "PCI DSS 4.0.1" in Compliance framework

  4. Step 4

    Locate Download CBOM

  5. Step 5

    Open /settings

  6. Step 6

    Select "SOC 2" in Framework

  7. Step 7

    Select "Weekly" in Cadence

  8. Step 8

    Fill Recipients with "auditor@acme.test"

  9. Step 9

    Click Add schedule
