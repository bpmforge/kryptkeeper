Getting around

One console for every certificate, CA and job that needs a person.

Sign in, see what needs attention today, and jump to any certificate, CA or setting from the keyboard. The console works the same on a phone.

  2. See what needs attention

    Read the dashboard: what expires soon, what failed, and what waits on a person.

  3. Find anything from the keyboard

    Use global search and the command palette to jump straight to a certificate or page.

    7 steps

  4. Use it on a phone

    Open the navigation menu and browse the certificate list on a phone-sized screen.

    4 steps
