Draft a policy from plain English

Describe a rule in a sentence and review the policy it produces.

As pki-admin

  1. Step 1

    Open /templates

  2. Step 2

    Click Draft policy from text

  3. Step 3

    Fill Policy name with "no-rsa-2048"

  4. Step 4

    Fill Policy text with "Require hardware tokens for all keys. Maximum validity 90 days."

  5. Step 5

    A phrase the parser does not understand is listed under These phrases were not applied

  6. Step 6

    Click Back to text

  7. Step 7

    Fill Policy text with "RSA keys must be at least 3072 bits. Maximum validity 90 days."

  8. Step 8

    Click Parse

  9. Step 9

    Click Submit draft
