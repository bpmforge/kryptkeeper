Sign in and find your way around

Sign in, see how the sidebar groups the pages, and switch the theme.

As pki-admin

  1. Step 1

    Open the sign-in page

  2. Step 2

    Fill Email with "ada.admin@acme.test"

  3. Step 3

    Fill Password (the value is masked)

  4. Step 4

    Click Sign in

  5. Step 5

    The sidebar groups the pages: Today, Automation, Connect, Estate, Evidence, Analysis, Administration

  6. Step 6

    Click the Dark theme button

  7. Step 7

    Click the Light theme button

  8. Step 8

    Click Collapse sidebar

  9. Step 9

    Click Expand sidebar
