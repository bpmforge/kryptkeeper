Renew certificates

Renew a single certificate or a whole selection at once.

As pki-admin

  1. Step 1

    Open /certificates

  2. Step 2

    Click web.acme.test

  3. Step 3

    Click Renew

  4. Step 4

    Paste your CSR into CSR PEM

  5. Step 5

    Click Confirm renew

  6. Step 6

    Open /certificates

  7. Step 7

    Tick Select feature-flags.svc.acme.test

  8. Step 8

    Tick Select ml-inference.svc.acme.test

  9. Step 9

    Click Bulk renew

  10. Step 10

    Open Expiry Timeline to confirm the distribution shifted
