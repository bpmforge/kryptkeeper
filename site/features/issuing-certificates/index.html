Feature

Issuing certificates

A guided wizard for people, an approval step when it matters.

Issue from your own CSR or let the wizard generate the key locally. Templates that need approval route the request to a second person, and the requester can track it to the end.

How it works

  1. Issue with your own CSR

    Walk through the guided wizard with a CSR you generated.

    9 steps

  2. Issue with a generated key

    Let the wizard give you the openssl command so the key never leaves your machine.

    9 steps

  3. Request a certificate that needs approval

    Submit a request, watch it wait for approval, then collect the certificate.

    9 steps

  4. Browse and inspect certificates

    Filter the inventory and open any certificate's details.

    9 steps

  5. Export the inventory as CSV

    Download the filtered inventory for a spreadsheet or another tool.

    3 steps
