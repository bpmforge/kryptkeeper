Feature

Discovery and inventory

Find the certificates you didn't know about.

Scan your network, pull inventory from other CAs, and watch Certificate Transparency logs for certificates issued in your name. Risky endpoints are flagged and can be accepted as risk with approval.

How it works

  1. Run a discovery scan

    Scan a range and read what was found.

    8 steps

  2. Add discovery sources

    Tell discovery which clusters, endpoints, domains and cloud accounts to watch.

    7 steps

  3. Inspect an endpoint and accept a risk

    Open a risky endpoint and propose a risk exception for approval.

    6 steps

  4. Sync certificates from another CA

    Pull the inventory of another CA into one view.

    4 steps

  5. Monitor Certificate Transparency

    See certificates issued for your domains by anyone.

    3 steps

  6. Review issuance anomalies

    Spot unusual issuance once the scheduled scan has run.

    4 steps
