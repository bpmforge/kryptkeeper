Set up ACME for a server

Create a provisioner and EAB key, then point an ACME client at it.

As pki-admin

  1. Step 1

    Open /acme

  2. Step 2

    Click New provisioner

  3. Step 3

    Fill Name with "internal-acme-2"

  4. Step 4

    The filled provisioner dialog

  5. Step 5

    Click Save

  6. Step 6

    Locate Copy directory URL for internal-acme-2

  7. Step 7

    Click EAB keys

  8. Step 8

    Click New key

  9. Step 9

    Fill Label with "cert-manager-prod"

  10. Step 10

    Click Create key
