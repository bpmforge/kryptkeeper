Set renewal policy per target

Choose a target's renewal method and threshold, set a group threshold, and clear an override.

As pki-admin

  1. Step 1

    Open /targets/renewal-policy

  2. Step 2

    Select "ACME" in web-01 renewal method

  3. Step 3

    Fill web-01 auto-renew threshold days with "21"

  4. Step 4

    Click Save next to the web-01 renewal threshold

  5. Step 5

    Click Clear override to go back to the default threshold

  6. Step 6

    Fill Group name with "prod-web-canary"

  7. Step 7

    Fill Group threshold days with "14"

  8. Step 8

    Click Save group
