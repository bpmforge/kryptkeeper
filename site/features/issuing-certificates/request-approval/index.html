Request a certificate that needs approval, then track it

As pki-admin

  1. Step 1

    Open /certificates/issue

  2. Step 2

    Select "Acme Issuing CA" in Certificate authority

  3. Step 3

    Select "Production Wildcard (needs approval)" in Issuance template

  4. Step 4

    Fill Subject common name with "*.pay.acme.test"

  5. Step 5

    Fill Subject alternative names with "*.pay.acme.test"

  6. Step 6

    Paste your CSR into CSR PEM

  7. Step 7

    Click Finish

  8. Step 8

    Open /my-requests
