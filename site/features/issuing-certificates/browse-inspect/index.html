Browse and inspect certificates

Filter the inventory and open any certificate's details.

As pki-admin

  1. Step 1

    Open /certificates

  2. Step 2

    Fill Search certificates with "acme"

  3. Step 3

    Select "Acme Issuing CA" in Filter by CA

  4. Step 4

    Select "Active" in Filter by certificate status

  5. Step 5

    Click Apply filters

  6. Step 6

    Click Unowned only

  7. Step 7

    Click lab-switch.acme.test

  8. Step 8

    Scroll down the detail card for risk score, fingerprint, SANs and ownership

  9. Step 9

    Click Back to list
