# Design Principles

## Purpose
Help enterprise PKI and crypto teams identify risk, automate routine certificate work, and prove control over their infrastructure.

## Aesthetic Direction: Industrial / Utilitarian
This interface should feel like a control room for critical infrastructure: dense, precise, and operational. Industrial/utilitarian fits the mental model of PKI owners, security ops, and auditors better than a soft SaaS dashboard because the work is about triage, provenance, and action — not browsing.

## The One Memorable Thing
A persistent “risk command rail” that always shows what is most urgent: expiring certificates, risky endpoints, failed notifications, and queue backlogs.

## Tone
- Voice: authoritative, concise, factual
- Density: balanced-to-dense
- Motion: subtle and functional

## Anti-Patterns (DO NOT)
- Generic SaaS card grids with equal-weight summaries
- Decorative gradients, especially purple-on-white
- Cute iconography or emoji-driven status cues
- Overly playful motion or bounce effects
- Hiding operational actions behind vague labels
- Treating alerts as passive notifications instead of work queues
- Using Inter, Roboto, Arial, or system-ui as the primary font
