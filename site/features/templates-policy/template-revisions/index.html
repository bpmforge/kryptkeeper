Edit a template

Open a template, change its validity period and save the change.

As pki-admin

  1. Step 1

    Open /templates

  2. Step 2

    Click tr

  3. Step 3

    Click Edit

  4. Step 4

    Fill Validity period with "60d"

  5. Step 5

    Click Save changes
