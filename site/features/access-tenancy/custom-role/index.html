Create a least-privilege role

Build a role with only the permissions a job needs.

As pki-admin

  1. Step 1

    Open /roles

  2. Step 2

    Click New role

  3. Step 3

    Fill Role name with "cert-reader"

  4. Step 4

    Fill Description with "Read-only access to certificates and reports"

  5. Step 5

    Tick certificate.read

  6. Step 6

    Tick report.read

  7. Step 7

    Tick audit.read

  8. Step 8

    Click Create role

  9. Step 9

    Creating a role with a name that already exists shows an error
