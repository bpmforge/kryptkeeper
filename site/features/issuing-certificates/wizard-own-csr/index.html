Issue with your own CSR

Walk through the guided wizard with a CSR you generated.

As pki-admin

  1. Step 1

    Open /certificates

  2. Step 2

    Click Issue certificate

  3. Step 3

    Select "Acme Issuing CA" in Certificate authority

  4. Step 4

    Select "Web Server (90 day)" in Issuance template

  5. Step 5

    Fill Subject common name with "web.acme.test"

  6. Step 6

    Fill Subject alternative names with "web.acme.test"

  7. Step 7

    Paste your CSR into CSR PEM

  8. Step 8

    Click Finish

  9. Step 9

    Fill Search certificates with "web.acme.test"
